Our Menu

Five options priced per person — each dish is costed differently based on ingredients. The lineup rotates seasonally, so check back for what's current.

01 — Signature Bestseller

Crispy Egg Rolls

Golden-fried egg rolls packed with seasoned pork, glass noodles, and fresh vegetables. Each roll is hand-wrapped and fried to order, served with our house sweet chili dipping sauce. A crowd favorite every time.

$14 / person
Min. 10 guests  ·  Subject to change with rotation
🍜
02 — Signature

Phở Noodle Soup

Slow-simmered broth with rice noodles, tender beef, fresh herbs, bean sprouts, and lime. Vietnamese comfort in every bowl.

$18 / person

03 — Signature

Gỏi Cuốn (Spring Rolls)

Fresh rice paper rolls filled with shrimp, vermicelli noodles, herbs, and lettuce. Paired with rich peanut dipping sauce.

$12 / person

04 — Signature

Bánh Mì Sandwich

Crusty baguette layered with seasoned meat, pickled daikon, jalapeños, cilantro, and house aioli. Bold flavors, perfect grab-and-go.

$13 / person

Cơm Tấm (Broken Rice)

Vietnamese broken rice served with grilled pork chop, shredded pork skin, steamed egg meatloaf, and house fish sauce.
